A portfolio can easily become a recurring major project: collect enough material, rebuild everything at once, then let it fall quiet again. NEXTFIELD tries another approach: design the site itself as a structure that can be updated continuously.

## Three different speeds of content

A project needs reasonably complete context, process, and outcome. A note may stop at one tested conclusion. An experiment might answer only a small question. These should not all be forced into the same content template.

The homepage is therefore a map rather than a list sorted by publication date. A visitor can enter through a project, a note, or an experiment.

## Make "unfinished" a normal state

The `Now` section, Build Log, and laboratory deliberately show work in progress. The site does not need to pretend that every question has already been settled, and each area leaves a clear opening for the next update.

> A structure that can be revised again and again is closer to the real creative process than a page declared finished once.
